This phylogeny shows the evolutionary relationships of **Orthohantavirus andesense** sequences.

Hantaviruses are rodent- and shrew-borne viruses with a tri-segmented negative-sense
RNA genome, comprising the **L** (large, RNA-dependent RNA polymerase), **M** (medium,
glycoproteins), and **S** (small, nucleocapsid) segments. Because the segments evolve
largely independently, a separate tree is built for each species and segment.

Sequences and associated metadata are downloaded from
[Pathoplexus](https://pathoplexus.org).
Trees are inferred with [IQ-TREE](http://www.iqtree.org/).

This dataset was produced with the
[Nextstrain hantavirus workflow](https://github.com/nextstrain/hantavirus).
